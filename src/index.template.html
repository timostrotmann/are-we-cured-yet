04 / OUR METHODOLOGY

Medical progress is a team effort. We track newly discovered or designed AI-origin medicines. Repurposed drugs are excluded from the approved-drug dataset and both counters. Research updates are kept in a separate news dataset.

01 What counts as a cure?

A disease counts only with clinical evidence of a durable curative outcome for a defined patient population and a documented AI role in a new treatment’s discovery or design. Repurposed drugs are excluded. Symptom relief, a promising experiment, remission alone, or an approval does not automatically qualify. Zero means no qualifying record in this dataset; it is not proof that AI has had no clinical benefit.

02 What counts as an approved drug?

We count distinct newly discovered or designed active substances with an FDA-approved therapeutic indication and a documented AI contribution to discovery or molecule design. Each substance counts once. Repurposed drugs — existing medicines identified for a new use — are excluded, even when AI helped identify that use. They are not included in the approved-drug dataset or either counter. Emergency authorization, trial clearance, and breakthrough designation do not count as marketing approval.

03 What do the numbers cover?

This is a curated dataset, not a global census. There is no single comprehensive, standardized “AI-created medicines” registry. Each counted example must have inspectable evidence. The counters are calculated from the qualifying entries in the current dataset. Research news is stored separately and never increases either counter. A treatment approval does not establish a cure.
